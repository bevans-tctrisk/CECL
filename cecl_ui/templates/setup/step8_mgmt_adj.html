Negative Share Provision Optional · Data-derived

A reusable Other Allowance Consideration for negative shares. Each quarter the model recomputes a life-of-loan loss rate — trailing months of net charge-offs (charge-offs − recoveries) ÷ the current negative-share balance — and applies it to the balance pulled from the current-month extract. The credit union sends a separate negative-share balance extract and a charge-off / recovery file; point the fields below at the folder that holds them.

{# ── Unfunded Commitments (data-derived) ────────────────── #} {% set uc = state.unfunded_commitment or {} %}

Unfunded Commitments Optional · Data-derived

Unfunded commitments are the undrawn dollars on lines of credit (LOCs, credit cards, HELOCs) that are not unconditionally cancelable. Enter the loan type codes the credit union flagged as not unconditionally cancelable. Each quarter the model sums the undrawn credit (Credit Limit − Current Balance) for those codes from the loan extract, groups it by pool, and applies that pool's ACL loss rate — producing one Unfunded Commitments – Pool Name row per pool.
