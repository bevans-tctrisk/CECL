# AI video prompt — Barbecue Sauce darts animation

For Sora / Veo / Runway / Kling. Sequence: two darts into triple 20, third
into the bullseye, title fades in, cross-fade to the shirt.

## Main prompt (8-10 seconds)

Flat vintage illustration style, matte black background, muted cream,
brick-red, and forest-green palette. A classic sisal dartboard fills the
frame, centered, rendered in clean flat vector shapes with no gradients or
photorealism.

- 0-2s: The board hangs alone in darkness, subtle ambient stillness.
- 2-3s: A dart with red-and-blue flights whips in from off-screen right and
  slams into the TRIPLE 20 band at the top of the board — quick impact
  shudder, dart quivers and settles at a slight angle.
- 3-4.5s: A second identical dart flies in and lands beside the first, also
  in the triple 20 — the two darts sit nearly parallel, angled apart like a V.
- 4.5-6.5s: Beat of stillness. Then the third dart arcs in slower, more
  deliberate, and buries itself dead center in the red BULLSEYE. The board
  gives one satisfying shudder. Everything holds.
- 6.5-8s: The words "Barbecue Sauce" fade in below the board in heavy cream
  slab-serif lettering.
- 8-10s: The entire scene gently cross-fades into a product shot: a black
  t-shirt on a plain dark background bearing this exact design — dartboard,
  three darts, "Barbecue Sauce" beneath.

Camera: locked, straight-on, no camera movement. Impacts feel weighty but
the style stays flat and graphic throughout — 2D animation, not 3D. No
people, no hands, no text other than "Barbecue Sauce."

## Short version

Flat 2D vintage illustration, black background. Three darts fly into a
dartboard one at a time: first two hit triple 20, third hits bullseye with a
satisfying shudder. Cream slab-serif text "Barbecue Sauce" fades in below.
Locked straight-on camera, muted cream/red/green palette, weighty impacts,
no people.

## Production notes

1. Use IMAGE-to-video, not text-to-video: feed the tool
   `found5_barbecue_sauce_print_BEVAN_4500x5400.png` (black version — video
   needs a background) as the reference or end frame so the board, colors,
   and Bevan type match the shirt exactly.
2. Most tools cap at 5-10s. If limited, cut the fade-to-shirt and do two
   clips: (1) darts sequence ending on the full design, (2) shirt product
   shot (use a real Printify mockup photo), joined with a cross-fade in any
   editor.
3. Generate 9:16 for Reels/TikTok/Shorts. Ask for "sound design: three dart
   thunks" on tools with audio (Veo, Sora) — the thunk-thunk...thunk rhythm
   is half the joke.
4. If the tool mangles the "Barbecue Sauce" lettering, generate with no text
   and overlay the real title from the transparent PNG in the editor.
